Four layers

What AlwaysConnected actually is

Not a social network. Not a generic chatbot. A stack that keeps connections alive between people, agents, and operating systems.

01

Connect

Presence, routing, and a single place that answers “is this still live?” across devices and agents.

02

Automate

Job ledgers, scheduled runs, and multi-agent control so work continues when you are not at the keyboard.

03

Operate

Status, retries, and a visible trail of what ran, what failed, and what is waiting on a human.

04

Build

Business infrastructure: public site, product surface, and a path to paid systems without a hosting bill on day one.
